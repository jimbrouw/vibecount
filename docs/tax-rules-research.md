# 2025/26 calculation research — M4 inputs and implementation notes

Checked 17 October 2026 against official material. This is engineering research, not accountant certification. The initial supported calculation/snapshot slice is documented in [tax-calculations.md](tax-calculations.md); remaining branches, independent tax review/HMRC corpus comparison and annual form mapping are still required. Exact ledger pence must be retained separately from HMRC worksheet rounding.

The [2026 technical specifications](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-technical-specifications-2026-for-individual-returns) provide the [calculation specification v1.5.1a](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69c26e8dcfa346b9d4704b3f/Calculate_Tax_and_NIC_MTR_version_1.5.1a.odt). Implementation must account for employment-by-employment rounding, allowance-taper rounding, separate Class 4 component rounding, relevant-earnings pension caps and Reg100 adjustments. Taxable P45 pay cannot establish Class 1 NICable earnings between PT and UEL; an unknown input must remain unknown. Never label a raw-pence planning calculation identical to an HMRC worksheet without verifying each convention.

Separate branches/inputs are required for savings/dividends, Gift Aid, student/postgraduate loans, marriage/blind allowances, child benefit, property/foreign income, non-residence, losses, basis-period transition profits, artist averaging, pension charges and termination complexities. The historic redundancy figure remains provisional until settlement evidence identifies the taxable components and prevents duplication with employment pay/withholding. No tax rates or example figures are automatically applied by the reviewed-fact foundation.
