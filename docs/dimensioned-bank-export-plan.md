# Standalone bank source export completion

Implemented locally after connected V3 parser/UI integration; see [implementation and verification](dimensioned-bank-exports.md). Foreign admission stays closed until this export branch and reviewed posting/reversal are proven together. The sections below retain the implementation contract and preceding architecture.

## Existing paths

- `/api/imports/[importId]/export`: `source-export.ts` produces `evidence-ledger/2`; `evidence-manifest-v3.ts` wraps V2 plus already implemented invoice-FX provenance as manifest/3. Its current source selection omits original signed units/ruleset, account currency and bank valuation history.
- `/api/reconciliation/export`: `dimensionedReconciliationExport` produces reconciliation/4. Foreign invoice amounts, actual GBP receipt lots and settlement/reversal history are already implemented. Its positive native receipt view is not complete foreign bank-source inventory.
- Frozen filing V5 artifacts already retain their own versioned bank context. Preserve their historical meaning and bytes; do not retrofit standalone export versions into saved packs.
