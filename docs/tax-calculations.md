# Versioned draft tax calculations

17 October 2026. Local M4 implementation, not a filing facility or completed handover. `/dashboard/tax-prep/calculation?year=2025` reads reviewed tax facts and the signed ledger, explains supported calculations, and saves immutable draft snapshots after explicit human review. Existing checklist and fact pages link to it.

## Calculation contract

The pure engine in `lib/tax/calculation.ts` uses checked integer pence, rational arithmetic and explicit rounding. `rules.ts` records the supported tax year, engine/rules versions, official sources and engineering review date. Independent tax review remains pending. The initial ruleset covers 2025/26, cash-basis, non-VAT, full-year UK sole traders, EWNI or Scotland, supported non-savings income, reviewed PAYE/termination components, RAS pension/Gift Aid band extension and allowance taper, ordinary/Reg100 Class 4, explicit Class 2 treatment, ordinary payments on account and actual allocated tax-payment credits.

The engine preserves exact ledger/PAYE values separately from whole-pound worksheet inputs. Its trace identifies each base, rounding operation, source IDs and rule references. Pension contributions never reduce trading profit. Net-pay/salary-sacrifice require confirmation that relief is already reflected in taxable pay. RAS eligibility/relevant earnings and unmodelled annual-allowance charges need explicit review; excess supported relief withholds liability. Pension assets and cash reserves have no contribution/payment input fields.

Vehicle mileage replaces linked signed actual costs, including related refunds. It is not multiplied again by business-use percentage. Actual-cost effects already apportioned in the ledger are retained once. Conflicting vehicle/adjustment costs, trading allowance plus other deductions, negative trading profits and unsupported tax branches stay unresolved. A reviewed add-back or capital/other deduction preserves source IDs and its separate calculation effect.

PAYE withheld tax retains pence and its sign; it is separate from NICable employment earnings. P45 taxable pay cannot establish PT-to-UEL Class 1 earnings needed for Reg100. Voluntary Class 2 is an explicit evidenced amount, not an assumed 52-week charge. Actual HMRC payment allocations reduce the balance, not assessed liability or the following-year POA base. POA minimum/percentage comparisons are strict, with each half rounded down to pennies and the operation disclosed. This draft convention is not annual form-mapping certification. Negative balances are potential repayments, not received cash.

Official research: [HMRC 2026 calculation/specification downloads](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-technical-specifications-2026-for-individual-returns), [payments on account](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/self-assessment-legal-framework/salf303), [2025/26 Scottish rates](https://www.gov.uk/scottish-income-tax/2025-to-2026-tax-year), [self-employed vehicle method](https://www.gov.uk/simpler-income-tax-simplified-expenses/vehicles). Parameter provenance and remaining branches are also recorded in `tax-rules-research.md`.

## Scope, completeness and ownership

Migration `20261017230000_tax_calculation_snapshots.sql` adds immutable `tax_calculation_scopes`, `tax_rulesets` and `tax_calculations`, plus an owner-valid calculation link on export audit rows. Scope revisions explicitly review NI exemption/election, pension/net-pay treatment, employment/termination/payment identities, vehicle coverage, trade/NI adjustments, Gift Aid, cash dates and assessment collection adjustments. Unknown answers remain provisional; supported values do not certify eligibility. Positive voluntary Class 2 needs an owned evidence document.

Scope revisions retain the exact tax review stamp. New facts, changed collection attestations, profile revisions or financial changes make the earlier scope stale. The existing profile's financial completeness and fact collection digests are checked separately. Missing/provisional/stale facts and unresolved observations withhold affected totals; no ready-to-file state exists. Incomplete results may be saved as drafts when that year's ruleset exists. Other years remain available for input review without borrowing 2025/26 rules to save a misleading calculation.

`read_tax_calculation_input` authenticates the owner and reads one database statement. A private explicit-owner helper produces a bounded-field projection of current profile/fact/section/scope revisions, records of every status, category meaning, signed source observations and current fact-source effects. Snapshot arrays have deterministic order. Bank observations retain account/provider/type/mask, original file/row hashes, parser-independent source IDs and decision versions. Manual rows remain honestly manual. Raw cells, descriptions, references, original filenames, object paths and key material are omitted. Linked record evidence uses integer `amount_pence`, even though the older immutable fact-source snapshot stores pounds. Foreign category metadata is filtered at the join, not merely labelled invalid after disclosure.

Private financial-stamp clones preserve the existing nested hash semantics without changing service JWT claims. A fourth helper mirrors the year-specific tax-review stamp. Their execution is revoked from public/anonymous/authenticated/service roles; only the checked definer entry points call them. A future change to the source financial/tax stamp must update these clones in a forward migration and retain parity tests.

## Save, retries and historical replay

The client submits reviewed input/result/rules hashes, year, request ID, reason and confirmation. The server authenticates, recomputes the current input/result/rules and compares the reviewed hashes. Clients cannot submit authoritative totals to the database. Only the trusted server can call the service-only capture RPC.

Capture takes the owner reconciliation advisory lock and brief `SHARE NOWAIT` locks on mutable accounts, records, categories, bank observations, imports and invoices. This covers legacy direct updates, category semantics, year moves and phantom inserts that do not share the newer owner lock. An active writer makes capture fail for reload; it does not wait in an inverted lock order. The locks briefly affect writers for all owners, so measure capture latency/contention before hosted rollout. They do not cover remote storage operations, which are outside the canonical normalized input.

After locking, capture reconstructs and compares the complete owner/year input, then stores exact canonical input/result/rules text, JSON, SHA-256 values, engine/rule versions, author, reason and request. Rules identifiers cannot acquire different content. Immutable snapshot/request history is the calculation decision audit. Capture writes zero financial records and creates no accepted/submitted state.

Exact request recovery precedes mutable input reads and capture locks, so a lost successful response is recoverable after later financial edits. A changed payload/reason/rules under that request is rejected. Saved pages and JSON export verify the retained bytes/hashes/versions and display the stored result. They never recalculate a historical figure from today's ledger. Export audit must succeed before download; the audit row links to the owned snapshot. Hashes establish retained-content integrity, not authenticity of the original evidence or professional approval.
