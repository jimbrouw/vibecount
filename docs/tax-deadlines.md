# Reviewed in-app tax-deadline reminders

17 October 2026. **Local model, immutable persistence, authenticated in-app review/history and audited export implementation.** No production migration, real financial posting, payment, filing, external reminder/calendar action or deployment occurred. Full M6 and hosted acceptance remain unfinished.

`lib/finance/deadlines/rules.ts` retains supported years, source URLs, review date/boundary, liability-year calendar offsets and a pinned engine version. `model.ts` validates exact source/choice shapes, generates seven distinct proposals and selected-year saved tax commitments, and evaluates reminder phases with date-only arithmetic. `snapshot.ts` freezes safe selected sources, retains canonical input/result/rules with SHA-256 and verifies exact historical replay against independently reconstructed source catalogs.

The implementation plan and official source review are in [tax-deadline-design.md](tax-deadline-design.md). General dates are proposals. Applicability starts unknown; paper/online/registration/tax-code eligibility and personal HMRC notices are not inferred from a saved calculation or pack. POA dates belong to the liability year, while an existing calculator's following-year POA illustration belongs to the next liability year. No forecast, reserve or profit becomes a demand.

## Financial and source meaning

Only tax commitments from the selected year in a verified retained finance revision supply payment amounts. The original plan ID, ordinal, as-of date and input/result hashes are retained. Unknown-year commitments become an unresolved count, not invented allocations. Other-year commitments, non-tax suppliers, pension/cash balances and scenario amounts are omitted. A nullable unpaid amount stays unknown, and zero does not automatically acknowledge the task. There is no new liability total: generic calendar proposals and actual commitments can describe the same event.

Saved commitments remain reviewed transcriptions as of the source date, not live HMRC balances or proof that payment remains outstanding. Future-dated source balances cannot activate a current commitment reminder. Their date/amount cannot be edited through reminder choices; a new cash-plan review is required. A personal notice can replace a general item's proposed date only with an explicitly selected owned document hash. Its date does not extend another item's payment deadline.

User decisions distinguish applicability, reviewed date basis, lead time and acknowledgement. Acknowledgement records handling the reminder; it establishes neither payment nor filing. All excluded/unknown items remain in the snapshot. Missing or duplicate rows, extra fields, unsupported years/versions, unsafe money, foreign evidence and ambiguous retained notice values reject.

## History, currentness and integrity

Historical verification requires an authoritative catalog reconstructed from the **referenced** immutable finance revision and retained owned notice metadata. Self-consistent rehashing of forged source/result bytes does not pass comparison to that catalog. Caller-submitted source catalogs cannot establish authority; the authenticated server reconstructs them before capture and replay. Notice content, filenames, object paths, keys and raw bank evidence are never part of this snapshot.

Current evaluation compares the latest plan identity/hashes and selected notice hashes with the retained sources. Changed/missing sources suppress active reminders and require fresh review. Unselected evidence additions do not make unrelated reminders stale. Historical bytes remain unchanged, including acknowledgements. With matching sources, phases are recalculated for today's London date without overwriting the saved result/date. After the engineering rules re-review boundary, open current reminders show a rules-review state with zero active count; historical verification still uses the original evaluation date.

The date engine handles whole civil days. A due-today item does not become past due before that day ends. UTC integer-day subtraction avoids DST drift; London timezone is used to choose today. Payment method/weekend guidance is a separate lead-time review, not an unreviewed rewrite of the statutory date. 2026/27 general recurrence remains labelled provisional planning guidance.

## Persistence, server boundary and workflow

Local migration `20261018040000_tax_deadline_revisions.sql` follows the full chain through merchant rollover. It adds immutable `tax_deadline_rulesets` and owner/year `tax_deadline_revisions`, source-plan/owner composite FK, expected ordinal, owner/request uniqueness, canonical bytes/hashes, author/reason/time and owner-composite export links. RLS permits owned revision reads and shared rules reads. Direct authenticated/service writes are revoked; capture is service-only and private explicit-owner helpers are not callable by authenticated/service roles.

The save action derives owner from `auth.getUser()`, requires exact human confirmation/reason and bounded typed fields, reconstructs complete owned catalogs and recomputes the displayed result/rules for the current London day. A changed read-back rejects. Exact completed requests recover before live context/date reads, using their retained original source revision/evidence and payload. A changed reused request rejects.

Capture serializes under the existing owner reconciliation lock, then resolves exact recovery before current source reads. Fresh requests check owner/year expected ordinal/context and current London date. SQL independently reconstructs the latest finance source reference, selected-year commitment identities/fields and unresolved-year count, compares keyed maps with duplicate checks (not locale-sensitive array order), and verifies selected notice ownership/hashes. It validates exact choices and safe envelope/result shapes; **the trusted server calculation and subsequent deterministic replay establish result correctness**. SQL does not independently calculate reminder phases. Rules and revision are inserted atomically; a late failure leaves no partial revision. No financial ledger, tax credit or communications state is changed.

`/dashboard/finance/deadlines` is linked from cash planning and dashboard navigation. It starts at the latest completed tax year when supported; unsupported defaults require an explicit supported-year choice. The editor shows original and reviewed dates, figures/words for saved commitment amounts, unknown applicability/amounts, evidence selection, lead days and explicit reminder handling. Changes to settings, reason or refreshed source props clear confirmation. Saved acknowledged/excluded settings remain in immutable history; changing a source resets the new proposals to unresolved instead of copying prior approval.

Current reminders recalculate phases on page load; no background/email delivery is enabled. Old saved phases are explicitly historical. Changed sources withhold active current reminders. Every history page can continue to earlier ordinals even with server caps. A failed source/history/context read suppresses the editor and current figures. Initial context includes all owned document metadata: an unrelated evidence addition can require reloading an **unsaved** review. Saved currentness depends only on selected notice hashes and the finance source, so that same addition does not stale a saved reminder.

`/api/finance/deadlines/export?id=…` authenticates, owner-scopes the selected revision, validates provenance, independently reconstructs its retained sources and replays its bytes. It requires an owner-linked export audit before returning private/no-store JSON. Later finance changes cannot substitute a new amount/date into historical export. No raw document content, private coordinates or filenames are returned.

## Migration, recovery and verification

This additive migration backfills no reminders, balances, financial approvals or tax facts. Before production, isolate the intended release from parked Phase 4, inspect linked migration/schema/grants/Storage state, replay the complete chain on clean and realistic legacy fixtures, and rehearse database/object/key restore. Secure capture needs existing server-only service-role configuration. To withdraw the feature, disable its navigation/read/save/export entry points while retaining rules, source plans, revisions and export history. New reviewed settings acknowledge/reopen reminders; do not delete immutable evidence as rollback.

The capture briefly takes a non-waiting table read lock on evidence metadata after the owner lock. Unrelated source writes can force a reload; assess contention under hosted load. Catalog validation currently bounds notice metadata to 10,000 owned documents and fails closed beyond that bound. These operational limits and browser component fixtures do not establish hosted authenticated behavior or full restore readiness.
