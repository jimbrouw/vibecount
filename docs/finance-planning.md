# M6 reviewed cash plans and forecasts

17 October 2026. **Local calculation, persistence, review dashboard and export implementation; full M6 and hosted acceptance remain unfinished.** No production migration, account connection, financial posting, send or deployment was performed.

`lib/finance/plan.ts` implements strict, browser-safe input validation and deterministic cash/forecast calculation. `lib/finance/rules.ts` pins separate 2026/27 planning parameters and official source URLs. `lib/finance/snapshot.ts` retains canonical inputs, safe linked-source metadata, results and rules with SHA-256 and deterministic replay. `/dashboard/finance` provides labelled balance/earmark/obligation/forecast fields, figures-and-words read-back, unresolved/shortfall displays and explicit human confirmation. Saving preserves draft reviewed inputs, not an approved financial record or tax return. `/api/finance/export?id=…` returns an audited, verified historical JSON snapshot.

## Financial meaning

Each physical asset appears once in an owner-wide plan. Cash may be signed for overdrafts; liabilities are separate positive debts. Pension and restricted assets never enter accessible cash. Business/emergency buffers and tax reserves are earmarks inside cash, not additional balances. Allocation capacity is checked per asset across all purposes and tax years, not separately for each year. Manual asset identity and completeness require explicit confirmation; linked account IDs cannot repeat.

Cash balances are user-transcribed as of one date, not inferred from historic receipts, invoices or an opening balance guessed from imported rows. Unknown balances remain null. A future or stale as-of date withholds current drawings. A missing pension asset balance does not create cash or prevent calculation of known cash; a missing cash/debt balance does withhold drawings.

For each tax year, protect the greater of its actual earmark and required tax cash. Current-year requirement is the greater of remaining forecast liability and known remaining unpaid HMRC obligations; prior/other-year requirements use their entered remaining unpaid demands. Sum these across years, then protect buffers, existing debts and separate unpaid non-tax commitments. This avoids adding the whole tax liability to cash already reserved for that same liability. An unresolved reserve year or unpaid obligation amount/year blocks drawings.

`current_free_cash_pence` may be negative to disclose a shortfall. `additional_drawings_ceiling_pence` is the non-negative minimum free cash across the current position and projected months, after the user's planned monthly drawings. It is conditional additional one-off headroom within that horizon, not a recommended salary, payment instruction or guarantee of future income. Existing debts are protected separately: cashflow `closing_cash_pence` is physical cash, while `protected_pence` includes debt protection.

## Forecast scope, timing and trace

The supported initial scenario is 2026/27, full-year UK sole trader, cash basis, non-VAT, eligible business costs, no other income, loans, loss relief, unmodelled allowances or NI exceptions. Jurisdiction is explicit. These are assumptions requiring confirmation, not inferred taxpayer facts. Missing/unsupported scope withholds tax results. Net and gross relief-at-source pension contribution bases are separate; pension eligibility, relevant earnings and absence of unmodelled allowance/MPAA charges require review. Pension contributions do not reduce trading profit or Class 4 profits. Below the Class 2 threshold, voluntary treatment is never guessed.

Annual turnover less twelve monthly business costs gives the annual scenario profit. Tax arithmetic reuses existing exact integer helpers with explicitly supplied planning rules. Results include a source-referenced trace for profit, pension cash/gross, allowance, income-tax bands, NI and actual-payment credit. `TaxRules` is now structural, and helper rule arguments accept their required subsets. The historical default rules, values and default 2025/26 calculator behaviour remain unchanged.

Official parameters were checked against [Income Tax rates](https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates), [Scottish rates](https://www.gov.uk/scottish-income-tax), [self-employed NI](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates), [NI parameter tables](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-allowances-national-insurance-contributions), [RAS contribution guidance](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm044220), [band extension guidance](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm056120) and [payments on account](https://www.gov.uk/understand-self-assessment-bill/payments-on-account). Engineering checks do not replace independent tax review.

Monthly projection timing is explicitly the fifth of each future month, through 5 April. From 6 April, twelve periods end on 5 May through 5 April. Receipts distribute annual turnover pennies exactly across these periods. Past periods are never added again to the current balance. Partial-period timing is an explicit planning assumption; users must assess its suitability. Monthly pension cash rounding is pinned per contribution so annual and monthly cash agree. Known payments reduce both physical cash and corresponding remaining protection once. Overdue unpaid obligations require timing review rather than pretending payment has happened.

The 2026/27 annual forecast can illustrate following-year POA on 31 January/31 July 2028. It does not establish current-year POA demands, which depend on the preceding assessment and belong in separately reviewed unpaid obligations. Reserve cash is never an actual-payment credit. Overpaid scenario tax does not invent a received refund. Billable day/rate targets round upwards; if entered capacity falls short of forecast turnover, drawings remain unavailable until the gap is resolved.

## Snapshots and source boundary

`prepareFinancePlan` takes an authenticated owner's server-read catalog. It validates all selected account/document ownership and retains only safe fields. Bank accounts can represent GBP cash; credit cards can represent liabilities; neither can be silently linked as pension cash. Documents retain ID/hash/type/size, never raw content, private object paths, filenames or keys. All manual/linked balances remain reviewed transcriptions, not provider-verified balances.

`verifyFinancePlan` checks canonical bytes/hashes, owner, known engine/rules, safe source closure and complete replay using the retained evaluation date. It returns detached decoded bytes; callers cannot mutate a previously verified return by editing the input row. Recomputed output hashes alone cannot forge a different result. Future engine/rule changes require a new version and retained verifier; unsupported versions fail closed rather than replacing historic figures with current calculations.

## Database capture and human review

Local migration `20261018020000_finance_plan_revisions.sql` adds immutable `finance_plan_revisions` and `finance_plan_rulesets`, owner-only revision SELECT, authenticated public-rule SELECT, service-only capture and owner-composite export links. One latest revision replaces the owner's whole plan across all years. Prior revisions remain immutable history; they are not parallel balances that can be added together.

`readFinanceReview` reads all safe catalog pages until empty, checks context before/after and rejects drift or incomplete pages. The page also checks latest ordinal/history/source consistency before showing a save form. Initial amounts/completeness are unknown, never synthetic defaults. Loading an older balance does not silently relabel its date as current. Editing inputs, adding/removing rows, changing sources/reason or receiving refreshed props resets final confirmation.

The authenticated action derives owner from cookies, requires human confirmation and a reason, validates submitted inputs and recomputes the preview against server-read sources and the current London date. It compares the complete displayed result and rule hash before privileged capture. Browser totals, owner IDs and sources cannot become authoritative by submission. Exact completed retries recover retained bytes before live reads, including after later revisions or source changes.

Capture holds the existing owner reconciliation advisory lock, resolves exact retries, then briefly takes `SHARE NOWAIT` on accounts/documents (and invoices in current coverage-aware capture). These locks cover legacy direct writes and phantom inserts. Under the locks, it checks the expected ordinal/context, validates JSON shape and cash capacity, reconstructs selected owned sources independently and compares the frozen metadata before inserting the next revision. A competing writer causes a fail/reload instead of waiting into a lock-order deadlock. A new request with stale context rejects; a completed exact request still recovers its original ID. No financial records, classifications, tax facts, actual credits, bank payments or sends are created.

Historical results are labelled with their saved balance/review date. New review uses current-date evaluation and withholds current drawings for stale balances. Export checks owner/revision/hash/source/rule/replay integrity and writes an owner-linked `record_exports` event before returning private/no-store JSON. A failed integrity check or audit releases no artifact. Pagination exposes earlier history using the last ordinal, including when a server caps below the requested page length.

## Migration and recovery notes

Apply only after the complete preceding migration chain through `20261018010000_record_category_integrity.sql`, after linked-state inventory, release isolation and backup review. This migration is additive with no financial backfill; historical receipts are never converted to opening cash. Capture needs the existing server-only Supabase service role configuration. Authenticated clients can read their revisions but cannot insert/update/delete authoritative snapshots. Neither clients nor service callers can invoke the private explicit-owner context helper directly.

Rehearse empty replay and real legacy upgrade, grants/RLS, actual PostgREST/Storage behavior and coordinated database/object/key restore before production rollout. Existing global read locks can briefly reject capture during an unrelated owner's source write; assess load/latency before broad release. To withdraw availability, disable the new navigation/review/save entry points while preserving revisions, rules and export history. Do not drop populated snapshot tables or rewrite their bytes as a rollback. This is not a complete restorable account backup; the older partial account-export route still explicitly omits other tables/evidence.
